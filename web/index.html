Controls

WASD move · Space jump · Ctrl (or double-tap W) sprint · Shift sneak (you won't walk off an edge) · mouse look · left click mine / attack · right click place, or hold to eat · 1–9 or wheel pick · Q throw away one held (Shift+Q the stack) · E inventory (click armor to wear it; tap a slot to throw it away) · H shop · M Mining Site · V view · B voice · T or Enter chat · F the rocket (stand on its pad, or click it) · Esc menu

Commands

Inventory

Tap two slots to swap them, or tap one to throw it away. The top row is your hotbar.
