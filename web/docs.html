Welcome to MineWorldCraftA shared planet of blocks that you play in your browser

MineWorldCraft is a block game that runs in your web browser at mineworldcraft.fun ↗. It works on a computer or a phone. There's nothing to install, and playing is free.

Everyone shares one small, round planet, 512 blocks around. Walk in a straight line and you come back to where you started. You mine, craft tools, build, fight monsters, talk to the villagers in town, and fly a rocket to three more planets: Deepvein, Cinderfall and Red Mesa.

The game's token is $MINE. It lives on Robinhood Chain, a blockchain network. You only need a crypto wallet to earn $MINE or to spend it (in the Shop, on rocket tickets and on revives).

Guest or signed in

Guest
Press Play and you're in. You can mine, build, craft, fight, chat and use voice chat on the home planet. Other players see you as "Guest-" and a number. Guests earn nothing, can't fly the rocket, can't deploy DEGENs for idle mining and can't pick a name.
Signed in
Log in with a browser wallet, such as MetaMask, Robinhood Wallet or Rabby. On a phone, open the game in your wallet app's own browser. Then press Sign in to earn: it's one free signature, not a transaction. From then on the world server counts what you earn, and you can pick a name, deploy DEGENs for idle mining, buy rocket tickets and pay to revive where you fell. (The Shop only needs a logged-in wallet with $MINE in it.)
Your bag lives in this browser: one bag for guests, and one for each wallet you log in with. Items are for playing with. They are never on-chain.

What earns $MINE

Once you're signed in, you earn $MINE for real play. Breaking the planet's own blocks pays: dirt and sand a tiny amount, stone and logs a little more, and ore the most. The rarer the ore, the more it pays. Beating monsters pays too. Blocks that players placed pay nothing when they're broken again, and neither do leaves or plants. If you have more I'M DEGENs, you can deploy up to 4 of them for idle mining: they earn by the hour while you're online, even while you rest. Nothing pays while you're offline.

Everything you earn on Deepvein pays double. Beating Ashgrim, the boss on Cinderfall, shares 150 $MINE among the signed-in players who hit it, by the damage each one did. The world server keeps the count: up to 1,500 $MINE a UTC day for what you earn yourself (wearing an I'M DEGEN can raise that), and a cap of its own for each deployed DEGEN. What all players earn together has no limit.

Turning earnings into $MINE

To get your earnings as $MINE in your wallet, you need a mining site. It costs 0.005 ETH, once per wallet, and it stays with that wallet. When you press Convert to $MINE, your site converts everything you haven't converted yet: 50% of it at level 1, and up to 100% at level 5 once you upgrade. The share your site doesn't convert isn't saved for later. It stays in the mining vault.

Each wallet can receive up to 3,500 $MINE a day this way, idle mining included, and all players together 35,000. That 35,000 is first come, first served: once the day's conversions reach it, converting opens again at 00:00 UTC. Whatever a day's limits don't cover waits for a later day.

A ticker across the top of the screen shows how much $MINE was mined today, the most that converts in a day (35,000 $MINE), when the daily limits reset (00:00 UTC), how much $MINE is left in the mining vault (the $MINE set aside for players), and how many mining sites are open. The title screen also shows how many people are mining right now.

Quick start

  • 1. Press Play on the title screen.
  • 2. Press M, or the Mining Site button, to open the Mining Site panel.
  • 3. Press Log in and pick your wallet. The game asks it to switch to Robinhood Chain, and to add the network first if it's missing.
  • 4. Press Sign in to earn and sign the message. It costs nothing.
  • 5. Press Open my mining site (0.005 ETH, once). Your wallet needs that much ETH on Robinhood Chain, plus a little for the network fee. You can earn without a site, but you need one to convert.
  • 6. Walk out of town, or type /wild in chat, and start mining. Beat monsters when night falls.
  • 7. Come back to the panel and press Convert to $MINE.

The ⛏ Mining Site & $MINE button on the title screen opens the same panel before you press Play. New to the game itself? Start with First steps.

  • Use 3 to 15 letters (A to Z), numbers or _. No spaces.
  • A name can't start with 0x.
  • Names that sound like the game or its staff are reserved, such as admin, mod, official, support, team, system, bot and dev. So is any name that starts with admin, official, moderator, support, staff, mineworld or guest. Underscores don't get around this.

First stepsFrom the town square to your first iron pickaxe

The town

You start in the town square, next to the fountain, with empty hands. The town is protected: nobody can mine or build within 64 blocks of the fountain, and no monsters come within 80 blocks of it. If you try to mine or build in town, a box pops up with two buttons: Go exploring (/wild) and Stay at spawn.

Places to know: the Mining Sites office just north of the fountain, the Smithy, where Garrick the armorer stands at the door and runs the shop, and the rocket on its launch pad in the north-east of town. There's also a bakery, a library, a trading post, a tavern, a barn, a town hall, a clock tower, a watchtower, houses, a flower garden, a pond and an orchard.

To leave, follow any of the four main roads past the town gates (52 blocks out) and keep going a little further. Or type /wild in chat to jump to a safe spot outside town.

Your first tools

  • 1. Find a tree outside town. Hold the left mouse button on its trunk. A log takes about 3 seconds to break by hand. Leaves sometimes drop a stick (about 1 in 8).
  • 2. Press E. Under your bag, the Crafting list shows every recipe. The ones you can't make yet are greyed out. Click a recipe to make it.
  • 3. Turn 1 log into 4 planks, then 2 planks into 4 sticks.
  • 4. Make a wooden pickaxe (3 planks and 2 sticks). Make sure it's in your hotbar, the top row of your bag, and pick it with 1 to 9.
  • 5. Dig down to stone. Stone breaks into cobblestone. Make a stone pickaxe (3 cobblestone and 2 sticks) and a stone sword (2 cobblestone and 1 stick).
  • 6. Find coal for torches, then iron ore for an iron pickaxe (3 iron and 2 sticks).

Ore drops its item as soon as you break it, so there's no furnace. Crafting needs no table or grid either: the list is all there is. In your bag, click two slots to swap them. The top row is your hotbar. Your bag holds 36 slots. When it's full, what you break is lost, and so is any crafted item that doesn't fit, so make room first.

Full bag? Open the inventory and tap a slot: Throw away gets rid of that stack, and Throw away all clears every stack of that kind at once (all your stone, say). Tools, armor, gold, diamonds and $MINE crystals ask "Sure?" first. Thrown-away things are gone for good. With the inventory closed, Q throws away one of what's in your hand and Shift+Q the whole stack (press Q twice for something precious).

To build, hold a block and right click where you want it. You can place blocks in air, in water or over a plant, but not in town. Blocks you place pay nothing when they're broken again. Glass drops nothing when you break it.

Food and hunger

Two sets of icons sit side by side just above your hotbar: hearts on the left for health, and bread on the right for food. Each set has 10 icons, and each icon is worth 2 points, so a full set is 20. You start full.

  • Healing: while your food is at 18 or more (9 icons), you heal half a heart every 4 seconds. Healing uses up some food.
  • Starving: at 0 food you lose half a heart every 4 seconds, down to half a heart. Hunger alone never knocks you out.
  • Sprinting needs at least 7 food (3½ icons). You also can't sprint while sneaking, eating or in water.
  • The food icons shake when you're down to 3 icons or fewer.

Everything you do uses a little food. One food point (half an icon) lasts for about:

  • 400 blocks of walking, or 40 blocks of sprinting
  • 80 jumps, or 20 sprint-jumps
  • 160 blocks broken
  • 40 swings at monsters

Healing half a heart costs ¾ of a food point.

  • Falling more than 3 blocks hurts: half a heart for each block past 3. Landing in water is safe.

Earning $MINEWhat pays, how much, and the daily limits

You earn $MINE by playing. You break natural blocks, defeat monsters and help beat Cinderfall's boss, and the world server counts what you earn on a ledger for your wallet. DEGENs you deploy for idle mining add to it too, while you're online. To turn those earnings into $MINE tokens, you need a mining site.

Sign in first

Guests can play, but guests earn nothing. To start earning:

  • Press M (or the Mining Site button on screen) to open the Mining Site panel. On the title screen, press ⛏ Mining Site & $MINE instead.
  • Press Log in and connect your wallet. The game switches your wallet to Robinhood Chain, and adds the chain first if your wallet doesn't have it.
  • Press Sign in to earn and sign the message. It's one free signature: it sends no transaction and costs no gas.

From then on, the world server counts your earnings, and your total for today shows on screen. A wallet earns in one place at a time: if you sign in somewhere else, your earnings count there instead, and the DEGENs you had deployed are recalled from the old place. Being knocked out never costs you earnings. You drop what you carry, but the ledger keeps everything it has counted.

Boosts raise your cap

The I'M DEGEN you wear boosts your mining while you play. To wear one, tap it under I'M DEGEN in the Mining Site panel. Its rarity and its level add up:

  • Daily cap: from +10% (a Common at level 1) to +150% (a Legendary at level 5). That's 1,650 to 3,750 $MINE a day.
  • $MINE: every block, monster and boss share pays from +5% to +70% more.
  • Speed: blocks break from +5% to +70% faster.

Boosts count only while you're signed in and wearing the DEGEN, and the world server checks on chain that it's yours. An NFT in your wallet earns nothing by itself. A DEGEN deployed for idle mining earns only while you're online, by its own daily cap, never with the boost of the DEGEN you wear. Across all slots, boosts top out at +150% speed, +150% $MINE and +300% daily cap (6,000 $MINE a day). For now only the DEGEN slot is live; the other slots (Skin, Companion, Aura, Vehicle) show Coming soon.

The world server checks every earning

Real blocks
The server builds the same world from the same seed, so it knows what every block was. It pays only for a natural block that is really there and has an open side.
Reach
The block must be within reach of where the server last saw you, and you can't be standing inside a solid block.
Breaking speed
No block can break faster than the best pickaxe allows. Your speed boost is counted in.
Movement
If you move faster than anyone can, you earn nothing for 10 seconds. The same 10-second pause follows connecting, signing in, a jump like /wild, and a rocket flight.
Monsters
The server spawns every monster. Hits can't come faster than a sword swings, or harder than the best weapon hits.
Protected places
Nobody can break or place blocks in the town (64 blocks round the fountain), within 20 blocks of a zone's landing pad, or in Cinderfall's arena and on the path to it. Open chat (T or Enter) and type /wild for a safe spot far from spawn (in a zone, far from its pad). You can use it every 30 seconds.
Idle mining
The world server pays idle mining itself, every 5 minutes, and only while your game is connected and signed in. It checks on chain that each deployed DEGEN is yours when you deploy it, and every 10 minutes after.

Mining sitesTurn your earnings into $MINE

Your earnings live on the world server's ledger. They become $MINE tokens only when you convert them at your own mining site. You can play and earn without a site, but you need one to convert. The ledger keeps your lifetime total, so earnings from before you open a site convert later too.

Open your site

  • Open the Mining Site panel (M) and press Log in.
  • Press Open my mining site. It costs 0.005 ETH, once, plus a little gas. The ETH goes straight to the project treasury.
  • Each wallet can open one site. It's yours for good, and it can't be moved to another wallet.
  • A new site is level 1, so it converts 50% of what you earn.

Convert to $MINE

Press Convert to $MINE in the Your earnings box. You need to be signed in to earn (see Earning $MINE) and have earnings you haven't converted yet. The reveal

DEGENs were minted sealed, so nobody could see a look while minting. After the sale closed, the team published a secret it had locked into the contract before the mint. At launch all three moves were used: the lock-in transactions sent from the team's wallet didn't go through in time, so each window passed. The fourth window was locked in by a separate wallet 1.4 seconds after it opened. The contract shows it (entropyMoves is 3).

Idle miningYour other DEGENs earn for you while you're online

Have more than one I'M DEGEN? You wear one and mine with it yourself. Up to 4 others can be deployed for idle mining. Each one earns $MINE little by little, all day, as long as you're online, and it all goes into your wallet's total. You don't have to be busy: the game just has to be open and signed in.

Deployed DEGENs don't appear in the world. They don't walk, dig or need pickaxes, and nobody can hit them. You see them in the Mining Site panel and in a line by your earnings at the top left. When you go offline, they stop earning.

Deploy a DEGEN

  • Sign in to earn first (Mining Site panel, M). Guests can't deploy DEGENs.
  • Open the Idle mining box in the Mining Site panel. It lists your DEGENs, all but the one you wear, with what each one earns a day and an hour.
  • Press Deploy next to a DEGEN. The world server checks on chain that it's yours, and it starts earning.
  • Press Recall to stop it. It's paid for its time up to that moment first.
  • This browser remembers which DEGENs you deployed. After a reload, or the next time you sign in here, the game deploys them again by itself.

Which DEGENs can be deployed

  • A DEGEN in the wallet you signed in with. The world server checks on chain when you deploy it, and again every 10 minutes. A DEGEN that has left your wallet is recalled.
  • Not the DEGEN you wear.
  • Not one you wore while earning today. The other way round too: a DEGEN that idle-mined today can't be worn until tomorrow (00:00 UTC).
  • Not one that's deployed somewhere else. If a DEGEN you just got is still deployed by its last owner, deploying it recalls theirs.
  • At most 4 at once, and at most 4 different DEGENs in a UTC day. You can recall a DEGEN and deploy the same one again, but a 5th different DEGEN that day is refused. Swapping in fresh DEGENs doesn't add more caps.

  • Paid every 5 minutes: the world server pays idle mining into your wallet's total on the ledger every 5 minutes, and when you recall a DEGEN. It converts like your own earnings, at your mining site's rate.
  • Counted for the DEGEN: a deployed DEGEN's day is counted for that DEGEN, not for your wallet. A DEGEN that already earned today keeps its count if it moves to another wallet. At its cap, it stops until 00:00 UTC.
  • Your cap stays yours: idle mining never uses up your own daily cap. When you reach yours, your deployed DEGENs keep earning to theirs.

  • Knock-outs don't stop it: your deployed DEGENs keep earning while you're down.
Converting has its own limit: your wallet can receive at most 3,500 $MINE a day from your mining site, whoever earned it. With idle mining you can earn more than that in a day. The rest stays on the ledger and converts on later days. The Idle mining box says so too.

Only while you're online

Your deployed DEGENs earn while your game is connected to the world and signed in. You don't have to move: standing still, chatting, or leaving the game open in a background tab all count. The game checks in with the world server by itself about every 45 seconds.

Offline, nothing pays. You're offline when you sign out, close the tab or the browser, lose your connection, or your phone goes to sleep. On a phone, the browser usually pauses the game when you switch to another app, and that counts as offline too. If the world server hasn't heard from your game for 2½ minutes, it stops paying. Time away is never paid later. When you're back and signed in, the game deploys your DEGENs again and they carry on.

The world server checks every hit on Ashgrim. You must be close to it, and you can't hit faster than a sword swings. To get a share of the $MINE, sign in to earn in the Mining Site panel (M) before the fight.

Red Mesa, the PvP Zone

  • This is the only place where players can hurt each other. Anywhere else, other players can't hit you.
  • Never on the pad: nobody can fight within 20 blocks of the landing pad's middle.
  • To attack, aim at a player and left-click (on a touch screen, tap ⛏), just as you would a monster. The world server checks every hit: you must be close, and you can't hit faster than a weapon swings.
  • Armor counts. It takes its share of every hit, and each piece you wear loses some durability.
  • A beaten player drops everything they carry, their whole inventory plus the armor they wear, in a loot chest where they fell. Then they're sent home. The chest stays in Red Mesa for 5 minutes, and the first player to open it takes everything. Everyone in Red Mesa sees who beat whom.
Only bring into Red Mesa what you're ready to lose.

Knock-outs and revivesWhat you drop, and how to get it back

When your health reaches zero, you are knocked out. A fall, monsters, the boss in Cinderfall or falling out of the world can do it. So can other players, but only in Red Mesa, and never on its landing pad. Hunger alone never knocks you out: an empty food bar hurts you only down to half a heart.

Your loot chest

  • Everything you carry, plus the armor you wear, drops in a loot chest where you fell. Tools and armor keep their wear.
  • The chest stays for 5 minutes. Its label says whose loot it is. Point at it to see the time left.
  • Anyone on that planet can open it, you included. Walk up close (a few blocks away), point at it and click. The first player to open it takes everything.
  • Make room first: anything that doesn't fit in your bag is lost.
  • You have at most one open chest. If you are knocked out again while carrying something, your new chest replaces the old one, and anything still in the old one is gone.
  • After several knock-outs in a row, the world server makes no chest for a short while, and you keep what you carry.
A knock-out never touches your $MINE earnings, your wallet or your NFTs. Only the items you carry drop.

The trade box in the Mining Site panel buys and sells $MINE with ETH or IMD. IMD is the token $MINE is paired with in its pool. Anyone can collect them, and they always go to the fee wallet.

Nothing pays while you're offline

$MINE comes only from the game: from what you do in the world, and from idle mining by the DEGENs you deploy while you're online. When you're offline, nothing pays, and time away is never paid later. A DEGEN in your wallet earns nothing by itself, and NFT boosts only add to what you do while you play.

Fixed forever

  • The token: the 21,000,000 cap. Its ownership is renounced, and it has no proxy, no pause, no blocklist and no transfer tax. Its only minter is the mining vault, set once.
  • The mining vault: at most 35,000 $MINE a day and 10,500,000 in all. It can't be upgraded. Its owner can replace the rules contract (MiningSites) only in the open, two days after proposing the new one.
  • The pool: the locked launch liquidity and the 4% fee. The hook's ownership is renounced: only the current fee wallet can change where the fees go.
  • I'M DEGEN: the art, the rarities and the 5% royalty rate.

What the owner can tune

Three contracts are upgradeable proxies (UUPS), owned by the project wallet. Every site, level and total is kept across upgrades.

  • MiningSites: the site price (never zero), the upgrade costs (at most 1,000,000 $MINE a level), the rates (10% to 100%, never lower for a higher level), the burn share, and more levels (up to 20; levels are never removed). Also the daily limits (at most 3,500 a wallet and 35,000 for everyone), the voucher key, and pausing conversions.
  • CrafterLevels: DEGEN level costs (at most 2,000,000 $MINE a level), the burn share, and more levels (up to 20).
  • MinePayments: each payment kind's burn share (0% to 100%), and new kinds of payment (up to 32 in all).
  • All three: where the treasury is, and the code itself.
  • The game itself: earning rates, the daily earn caps, the idle mining rules, and shop, ticket and revive prices live in the game's code. An update to the site and world server can change them. The source is public on GitHub ↗.
Whatever code runs in those contracts, nobody, the owner included, can mint more than the vault allows: at most 35,000 $MINE a day. Ownership moves in two steps and can't be renounced. It can be handed to a multisig or a timelock.

Who controls what

  • No owner: the $MINE token and the pool hook gave up their ownership for good. Nobody can change them, and nobody but MineVault can mint $MINE. One thing is left: the fee wallet can point its own trading fees to a new address. Nobody else can.
  • The owner wallet 0x6e85Dc9f094b7DD35a2A981AbBd0AC9C496670eD owns MineVault, the three game contracts (MiningSites, MinePayments, CrafterLevels) and I'M DEGEN. The same wallet is also the treasury and the fee wallet. It's a regular wallet with one key, not a multisig.
  • The owner can change the game contracts' settings within limits written in their code. That covers the site price, the site rates (each 10% to 100%), upgrade and level costs, burn shares, the treasury, the world server's signing key and the claim caps (at most 3,500 $MINE per wallet a day and 35,000 for everyone; today 3,500 and 35,000). It can also pause conversions to $MINE.
  • The owner can upgrade the code of those three contracts, and an upgrade can change their limits too. Setting changes and upgrades take effect at once, with no waiting period.
  • Whatever the owner does, MineVault never mints more than 35,000 $MINE a day or 10,500,000 in all.
  • The owner can swap out MiningSites, the contract that asks the vault to mint, but only in public and after a two-day wait.
  • These contracts can't give up their owner, and that's on purpose. The game's rules need to stay manageable, and I'M DEGEN needs an owner so its OpenSea page and links stay editable.
  • The world server decides what you earned and signs your vouchers. The daily caps in MiningSites limit what vouchers can pay out.

What can never change

Supply cap
$MINE can never pass 21,000,000. Every mint counts toward it, and burned $MINE is gone for good.
Vault limits
MineVault mints at most 35,000 $MINE per UTC day and 10,500,000 in all. It isn't upgradeable.
Locked liquidity
The liquidity added to the pool at launch (10,500,000 $MINE) is owned by the hook, and the hook has no way to remove it.
4% fee
Every swap pays 4% of its IMD side to the fee wallet. The rate is fixed in the hook's code.
DEGEN art
Each DEGEN's picture and looks are fixed. CrafterArt draws them, and it has no owner and no settings. I'M DEGEN can't switch to a different renderer. The owner can only add extra traits, like the Level, through an add-on contract.
Royalty rate
The I'M DEGEN royalty is fixed at 5%. It goes to the treasury (the owner can move the treasury, not change the rate).

Stay safe

  • Only play at mineworldcraft.fun ↗. Bookmark it instead of clicking links that strangers send you.
  • Before you approve or trade anything, check the contract address against this page. Every approval the game asks for names one of the contracts above. Your wallet should be on Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663).
  • The game never asks for your seed phrase or private key. Anyone who asks for your seed phrase is trying to steal from you.
  • Signing in to earn is one free signature, not a transaction. The message starts with "MineWorldCraft sign-in" and says it's free and sends no transaction.
  • Every $MINE payment in the game (the shop, tickets, revives, site upgrades and DEGEN levels) approves exactly its price, never more. Step 1 approves the amount and step 2 pays it. Once step 2 goes through, nothing stays approved. If your wallet asks you to approve more than the price, stop.
  • When you sell $MINE in the trade box, your wallet may ask you to sign a permit instead of approving. It's free, and it lets the router take exactly the amount you sell, for 10 minutes. A free signature can still let a contract spend your tokens, so always read what you sign.
